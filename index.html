Protected pots

A pot reserves money that remains in your bank. Removing a pot instantly releases that amount into “Free now.” Spending belongs in cashflow only when money actually leaves the account.

The running balance follows real money in and out. Protected pots stay separate, so travel and food each count once.

Backup and restore

The app saves automatically in this browser. A backup protects your numbers when moving phones or clearing browser data.

Install on your phone

After the site is hosted, open it in Safari or Chrome and choose “Add to Home Screen.” Compass then opens from its own icon and works offline after the first visit.
